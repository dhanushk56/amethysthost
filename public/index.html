FAQ

Common questions

Do free servers really have no limits on time?

Free servers run 2GB RAM / 10GB NVMe storage forever, with no trial period and no credit card required. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any point without losing your world.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. Changing plans re-allocates your server's resources; your world files, plugins, and configuration are preserved.

What happens if a payment fails?

Your server is suspended (not deleted) until billing is resolved, so your world stays safe while you update payment details.

Which regions are available?

North America and Europe today, with more regions added as node capacity expands.
